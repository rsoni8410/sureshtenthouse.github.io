About Suresh Tent House

Built on the ground, not on promises — a name Kota families have called for their most important days.

Rooted in Kota

Suresh Tent House Enterprise Ltd. has grown alongside the neighbourhoods of Kota, setting up tents for everything from a small ghar ki puja to full multi-day wedding functions. Every job, big or small, gets the same attention — the tent has to hold, the lighting has to work, and the mandap has to look right in every photograph.

How we work

A booking usually starts with a phone call or a WhatsApp message about your date and venue. We visit the ground where possible, understand the guest count and the theme you have in mind, and come back with a clear quote — what's included, what it costs, and when the crew arrives to set up. Nothing changes on the day of the function without you knowing first.

Why families call us back

Weddings don't get a second date. Our crew arrives early, tests the sound and lighting before guests arrive, and keeps a standby plan for weather — especially with our waterproof tent options during the monsoon. Word has carried the business from one family function to the next for years, and that trust is what we work to keep.

Areas we serve

Based in Kota and covering the surrounding areas — from home lawns and community halls to farmhouses and open wedding grounds. If you're unsure whether your venue is within reach, just ask on WhatsApp with your location.
