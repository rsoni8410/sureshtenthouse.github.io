What we set up

From a small tilak ceremony at home to a thousand-guest wedding lawn — here is everything Suresh Tent House brings to the ground.

Tents & shamiana

German hangar tents for large open lawns, Mughal-style tents for a regal look, and traditional cloth shamiana for smaller family functions. We measure your ground first and recommend a size that leaves room for movement, seating and the buffet line without feeling empty or cramped.

Chairs & tables

Steel banquet chairs with covers, round tables for family seating, long tables for the buffet, and cushioned sofa seating for the bride, groom and elders. Arranged and rearranged as your function moves from ceremony to dinner.

Mandap & stage decoration

Flower mandaps, fabric-draped mandaps and fully lit modern mandaps built around the colour palette you choose. We also build raised stages for sangeet performances, the reception seating and the entry photo point.

Lighting

Warm fairy-light canopies, hanging chandeliers over the mandap, LED walls for the stage backdrop, and gate lighting that makes the entrance visible from the road. Colour and brightness are adjusted for daytime versus evening functions.

Sound system

Speaker rigs sized to your lawn or hall, wireless collar and handheld mics for pheras, speeches and the pandit ji, plus DJ setup for sangeet and reception with clean, even coverage so no corner of the venue is left out.

Catering setup

Live counter placement, buffet line layout, crockery and cutlery arrangement, and coordination with your caterer's serving staff so food service moves smoothly even at peak dinner hour.

Waterproof tents

Sealed-joint waterproof shamiana with proper slope and drainage planning for the monsoon season, so an unexpected shower doesn't disturb the ceremony or the guests seated underneath.
